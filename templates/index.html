FAQs

Frequently asked questions.

What is MRK Agency and what makes it different?

MRK Agency is Pakistan's premier hybrid digital agency — a full-stack partner working with clients internationally, offering web development, software development, design, UI/UX, SEO, and in-house AI integration, delivered through a structured, portal-tracked process rather than ad-hoc freelance work.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes — MRK Agency works internationally as a distributed team, communicating primarily over WhatsApp and through the client portal so time zones don't slow projects down.

Is the AI assistant on this site actually built by MRK?

Yes — MRK AI is built in-house, not a third-party plugin, and is already live answering questions on this site.

What kind of support do you provide after launch?

Every package includes unlimited revisions, and you keep direct WhatsApp access to the team for ongoing support after launch.

How do I track progress on my project?

You get access to your own client portal, where each milestone is reviewed and approved before moving to the next stage — so you always know exactly where your project stands.
